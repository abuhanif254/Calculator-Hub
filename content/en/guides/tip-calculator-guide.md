# Tip Calculator: How Much Should You Tip?

You just finished a fantastic meal at a restaurant. The food was delicious, the service was excellent, and now the bill arrives. How much should you leave as a tip? Calculating tips — especially when splitting a bill between multiple people — can cause a surprising amount of confusion and even social anxiety.

Our free [Tip Calculator](/en/calculators/tip-calculator) makes this effortless. But first, let's learn the social norms around tipping so you always feel confident.

---

## 🍽️ The Standard Tipping Guide

Tipping customs vary by country and service type, but in the United States, these are the widely accepted norms:

| Service Type | Standard Tip |
|---|---|
| Restaurant (Sit-Down) | 15% – 20% |
| Exceptional Service | 20% – 25%+ |
| Fast Food / Counter Service | Optional (0% – 10%) |
| Bartender | 15% – 20% or $1–$2 per drink |
| Food Delivery | 15% – 20% of order |
| Taxi / Rideshare | 15% – 20% |
| Hotel Housekeeping | $2 – $5 per night |
| Haircut / Salon | 15% – 20% |

---

## 🧮 How to Calculate a Tip

The math for a standard 20% tip is straightforward:

**Tip Amount = Bill Total × Tip Percentage**

For a \$85 restaurant bill at 20%:
* Tip Amount = \$85 × 0.20 = **\$17.00**
* Total = \$85 + \$17 = **\$102.00**

When splitting with friends, divide the total by the number of people:
* Per Person = \$102 ÷ 4 people = **\$25.50 each**
