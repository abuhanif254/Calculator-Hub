# Ideal Gas Law Calculator: PV = nRT

If you inflate a balloon inside a warm house and then take it outside into the freezing snow, it will immediately shrink. Why? Because the temperature of a gas directly affects its volume and pressure. 

To understand exactly how these variables interact, scientists use the **Ideal Gas Law**. In this guide, we will explain the science behind the equation and how to use our free [Ideal Gas Law Calculator](/en/calculators/ideal-gas-law-calculator).

---

## 🎈 What is an "Ideal" Gas?

In the real world, gas molecules occasionally bump into each other and attract one another. However, these interactions are usually so tiny that they don't matter. 
For the sake of easy math, chemists pretend that gas molecules are infinitely small and bounce off each other perfectly without losing energy. We call this imaginary perfect gas an **Ideal Gas**.

---

## 🧮 The Ideal Gas Law Equation

The equation connects four fundamental properties of a gas:

**The Formula:**
**$PV = nRT$**

* **$P$ (Pressure):** The force the gas exerts on its container (usually in atmospheres or Pascals).
* **$V$ (Volume):** The amount of space the gas takes up (in Liters).
* **$n$ (Moles):** The amount of gas particles present.
* **$R$ (Ideal Gas Constant):** A special number that makes the math work. (If using atmospheres and liters, $R = 0.08206 \text{ L}\cdot\text{atm/(mol}\cdot\text{K)}$).
* **$T$ (Temperature):** The heat of the gas, which MUST be measured in Kelvin.
