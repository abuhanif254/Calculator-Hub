# How to Add and Subtract Time: The Ultimate Guide

In a world driven by schedules, deadlines, and billable hours, calculating time accurately is an essential skill. However, because our clocks are based on the sexagesimal (base-60) system, standard calculators are completely useless for time math.

In this guide, we will break down the rules for adding and subtracting hours and minutes, explain how to calculate payroll hours, and show you how to use our free [Time Calculator](/en/calculators/time-calculator) to completely eliminate math errors.

## 🕒 The Problem with Time Math (Base-60)

If you have 1.5 dollars and you add 1.5 dollars, you get 3.0 dollars.
If you have 1 hour and 50 minutes and you add 1 hour and 50 minutes, you do NOT get 3 hours. You get 3 hours and 40 minutes!

This happens because standard math uses a base-10 system (everything resets at 10, 100, 1000). Time uses a base-60 system:
* 60 seconds = 1 minute
* 60 minutes = 1 hour
* 24 hours = 1 day

Because the rollover points are different, you cannot use decimals interchangeably with minutes. **0.5 hours is 30 minutes, not 50 minutes.**

---

## ➕ How to Add Time Manually

To add two times together (e.g., 2 hours 45 minutes + 1 hour 30 minutes):

1. **Add the minutes together:** 45 + 30 = 75 minutes.
2. **Check for rollover:** Since 75 is greater than 60, you must subtract 60 and carry over 1 hour. (75 - 60 = 15 minutes left over).
3. **Add the hours together:** 2 + 1 + 1 (carried over) = 4 hours.
4. **Final Answer:** 4 hours and 15 minutes.

---

## ➖ How to Subtract Time Manually (Payroll)

Subtracting time is common when calculating how long someone worked (e.g., clocked in at 8:45 AM, clocked out at 5:15 PM).

1. **Convert to 24-hour military time:** 
   * 8:45 AM stays 08:45. 
   * 5:15 PM becomes 17:15.
2. **Subtract the minutes:** 15 - 45. Since you cannot subtract 45 from 15, you must "borrow" 1 hour from the 17 hours.
3. **Borrowing:** 17 hours becomes 16 hours. You add 60 minutes to the 15 minutes, making it 75 minutes.
4. **Re-subtract:** 75 - 45 = 30 minutes.
5. **Subtract the hours:** 16 - 8 = 8 hours.
6. **Final Answer:** 8 hours and 30 minutes worked.
