# How to Estimate Concrete for Your Next DIY Project

Pouring your own concrete patio or setting fence posts can save you thousands of dollars in contractor fees. But concrete is a race against time. If you mix too little, the first batch will start curing before you can mix a second, resulting in a weak, ugly "cold joint." If you order too much from a ready-mix truck, you are wasting money and must figure out where to dump the heavy excess.

In this guide, we will break down the math of concrete volume and show you how to use our free [Concrete Calculator](/en/calculators/concrete-calculator) to ensure you order the exact right amount for your project.

---

## 🧮 The Math: Understanding Cubic Yards

In North America, concrete is almost exclusively sold and measured in **cubic yards**, not square feet. 

A cubic yard is a block of material that is 3 feet long, 3 feet wide, and 3 feet deep.
*(3 x 3 x 3 = 27 cubic feet).*

**The standard formula for a rectangular slab is:**
1. Determine the length (in feet).
2. Determine the width (in feet).
3. Determine the depth/thickness (in inches).
4. Divide the thickness by 12 to convert it to feet.
5. Multiply Length × Width × Thickness(in feet) to get **Cubic Feet**.
6. Divide the Cubic Feet by 27 to get **Cubic Yards**.

*Example: A 10ft x 10ft patio that is 4 inches thick.*
* Thickness in feet: 4 / 12 = 0.333 ft
* Cubic feet: 10 × 10 × 0.333 = 33.33 cubic feet.
* Cubic yards: 33.33 / 27 = **1.23 cubic yards.**

---

## ⚠️ The Golden Rule: Account for Spillage (The 10% Rule)

No matter how perfectly you build your wooden forms, the ground underneath is never perfectly flat. A slab that you think is 4 inches thick might actually be 4.5 inches thick in a slight depression in the dirt. Furthermore, some concrete will always spill over the edges or get stuck inside the mixer.

**Contractors always add 10% to their final calculation to account for waste and uneven subgrades.**
* If your math says you need 1.23 cubic yards, you should actually order/mix 1.35 cubic yards. (1.23 × 1.10 = 1.35).
