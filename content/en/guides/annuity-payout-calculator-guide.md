# Annuity Payout Calculator: Securing Your Retirement

The biggest fear most people have about retirement is outliving their money. If you have $500,000 saved up, how do you know it will last for the rest of your life?

To solve this problem, many retirees purchase an **Annuity**. In this guide, we will explain what annuities are, how they protect your income, and how to use our free [Annuity Payout Calculator](/en/calculators/annuity-payout-calculator).

---

## 🛡️ What is an Annuity?

An annuity is a financial contract between you and an insurance company. 
You give the insurance company a massive lump sum of money upfront (e.g., $300,000). In exchange, the insurance company promises to pay you a guaranteed, steady monthly paycheck for a specific period of time—often for the rest of your life, regardless of how long you live.

It is essentially the opposite of a life insurance policy. Life insurance pays out when you die; an annuity pays out *while you live* to ensure you never run out of cash.

---

## 📈 Fixed vs. Variable Annuities

There are two main types of annuities:

* **Fixed Annuity:** The insurance company guarantees a specific interest rate. Your monthly payouts will never change. It is extremely safe, but your payouts might lose purchasing power over time due to inflation.
* **Variable Annuity:** The insurance company invests your lump sum into mutual funds. If the stock market goes up, your monthly payouts increase! However, if the market crashes, your payouts could drop significantly. 

*(Note: There are also "Indexed Annuities" that offer a middle-ground, capping your maximum gains but protecting you from total market losses).*
