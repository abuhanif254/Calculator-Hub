# How to Calculate Conception Date and Track Your Pregnancy

Knowing when you conceived is an exciting milestone. It helps doctors estimate your due date, track fetal development, and schedule important ultrasounds. 

In this guide, we will explore the science behind conception windows and how to use our [Pregnancy Conception Calculator](/en/calculators/pregnancy-conception-calculator) to find your exact dates.

---

## 🧬 The Biology of Conception

Conception occurs when a sperm fertilizes an egg. However, figuring out exactly *when* this happened requires understanding your menstrual cycle.

### The Ovulation Window
A typical menstrual cycle lasts 28 days. Ovulation (when the ovary releases an egg) usually happens around Day 14. 
* An egg only lives for **12 to 24 hours** after it is released.
* However, sperm can live inside the female body for up to **5 days**.

This creates a "fertile window" of about 6 days: the 5 days leading up to ovulation, and the day of ovulation itself. If you had intercourse during this window, conception most likely occurred on the day of ovulation (or the day after).

---

## 📅 How Doctors Date a Pregnancy

It is notoriously difficult to pinpoint the exact moment of fertilization. Because of this, medical professionals use a standardized method to date pregnancies: **LMP (Last Menstrual Period)**.

Instead of counting from the day you conceived, doctors count from the first day of your last period. 
* This means that on the day you conceive, you are already considered "2 weeks pregnant" by medical standards!
* A full-term pregnancy is considered 40 weeks from the LMP (or 38 weeks from the date of conception).
