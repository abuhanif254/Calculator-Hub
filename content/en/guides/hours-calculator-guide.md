# The Payroll Masterclass: Calculating Timesheets and Work Hours

If you are an hourly employee, your timesheet is your paycheck. However, calculating exactly how many hours you worked in a week can be surprisingly tricky due to unpaid lunch breaks, overtime rules, and the mathematical nightmare of converting base-60 minutes into base-10 decimals.

In this guide, we will break down the mechanics of payroll math, explain how to account for breaks, and show you how to use our free [Hours Calculator](/en/calculators/hours-calculator) to ensure your paycheck is always accurate.

---

## 🧮 The Minute-to-Decimal Conversion Trap

The number one mistake employees make when calculating their pay is treating minutes like decimals. 

If you worked 8 hours and 30 minutes, you did NOT work 8.30 hours! You worked eight and a half hours, which is **8.5 hours**. If you multiply your hourly wage by 8.3 instead of 8.5, you are cheating yourself out of money.

**How to Convert Minutes to Decimals:**
Divide your total minutes by 60.
* 15 minutes / 60 = 0.25 hours
* 30 minutes / 60 = 0.50 hours
* 45 minutes / 60 = 0.75 hours

If you clocked out at 5:42 PM, you worked an extra 42 minutes. (42 / 60 = 0.70). You add 0.70 to your total hours for the day.

## 🥪 Subtracting Unpaid Lunch Breaks

Most employers require you to take a 30-minute or 60-minute unpaid lunch break during an 8-hour shift. When calculating your total daily hours, you must manually subtract this time.

**Example Calculation:**
* Clock In: 8:00 AM
* Clock Out: 5:00 PM
* **Total Elapsed Time:** 9 hours (from 8 AM to 5 PM)
* **Minus Lunch Break:** Subtract 30 minutes (0.5 hours)
* **Total Paid Hours:** 8.5 hours

If you earn $20 an hour, your gross pay for the day is $170 (8.5 × 20).

---

## 📈 Understanding Overtime (Time and a Half)

In the United States, the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) requires employers to pay "time and a half" for any hours worked over 40 in a single workweek.

If your standard rate is **$20/hour**, your overtime rate is **$30/hour** (20 × 1.5).

* If you work 45 hours in a week:
* Regular Pay: 40 hours × $20 = $800
* Overtime Pay: 5 hours × $30 = $150
* **Total Gross Pay:** $950

*(Note: Some states, like California, also require overtime pay for any hours worked over 8 in a single day, regardless of the weekly total).*
