# Simple vs. Compound Interest: A Complete Guide to Growth

Albert Einstein reportedly called compound interest the "eighth wonder of the world." Understanding how interest works is the single most important lesson in personal finance. Whether you are paying interest on a loan or earning interest on an investment, the math will either work aggressively for you or aggressively against you.

In this guide, we will break down the two main types of interest, explain the exponential power of compounding, and show you how to use our [Interest Calculator](/en/calculators/interest-calculator) to forecast your financial future.

---

## ➖ What is Simple Interest?

Simple interest is calculated *only* on the principal amount (the original amount of money). You do not earn interest on any previously accumulated interest. 

### The Formula:
**Interest = Principal × Rate × Time**
*(I = P × R × T)*

* **Example:** You invest $10,000 at a 5% simple interest rate for 3 years.
* **Year 1:** $10,000 × 5% = $500
* **Year 2:** $10,000 × 5% = $500
* **Year 3:** $10,000 × 5% = $500
* **Total Interest Earned:** $1,500.

Simple interest is easy to calculate, but it is rarely used in the real world for savings accounts or long-term investments.

---

## 📈 What is Compound Interest?

Compound interest is calculated on the principal amount *and* on the accumulated interest from previous periods. It is "interest on interest."

This creates an exponential growth curve. Over a long period, compound interest will vastly outperform simple interest.

### The Formula:
**A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)**
*(Where A is the final amount, P is principal, r is rate, n is compounding frequency, and t is time).*

* **Example:** You invest $10,000 at a 5% interest rate compounded annually for 3 years.
* **Year 1:** $10,000 × 5% = $500 (Total = $10,500)
* **Year 2:** $10,500 × 5% = $525 (Total = $11,025)
* **Year 3:** $11,025 × 5% = $551.25 (Total = $11,576.25)
* **Total Interest Earned:** $1,576.25.

In just three years, compounding earned you an extra $76.25. Over 30 years, that difference becomes hundreds of thousands of dollars!
