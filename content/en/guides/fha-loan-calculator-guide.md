# The Ultimate Guide to FHA Loans: Requirements and Calculations

Buying a home is the largest financial transaction most people will ever make. If you don't have the traditional 20% down payment saved up, a Federal Housing Administration (FHA) loan might be your best path to homeownership.

In this guide, we will break down what an FHA loan is, the strict qualification requirements, and how to use our free [FHA Loan Calculator](/en/calculators/fha-loan-calculator) to estimate your true monthly costs.

---

## 🏛️ What is an FHA Loan?

An FHA loan is a mortgage issued by an FHA-approved lender and insured by the Federal Housing Administration (a US government agency). 

Because the government insures the loan, lenders take on less risk. This allows them to offer mortgages to buyers with lower credit scores and smaller down payments than conventional loans require.

### The Big Benefit: 3.5% Down Payment
The most famous feature of the FHA loan is the low down payment. If you have a credit score of at least 580, you can borrow up to 96.5% of the value of a home, meaning you only need to bring 3.5% as a down payment.

---

## 📋 FHA Loan Requirements

To qualify for an FHA loan, you must meet specific criteria:

1. **Credit Score:** 
   * 580 or higher = 3.5% down payment.
   * 500 to 579 = 10% down payment.
   * Under 500 = Generally not eligible.
2. **Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI):** Your total monthly debt payments (including the new mortgage) should generally not exceed 43% of your gross monthly income.
3. **Primary Residence:** You must live in the home. You cannot use an FHA loan to buy an investment property or a vacation home.
4. **Property Standards:** The home must pass a strict FHA appraisal to ensure it is safe, secure, and structurally sound.

---

## 💸 The Hidden Cost: Mortgage Insurance Premium (MIP)

Because you are putting down less than 20%, the FHA requires you to pay for Mortgage Insurance Premium (MIP). This protects the lender if you default. There are two parts to MIP:

1. **Upfront MIP:** This is 1.75% of the base loan amount. It is usually rolled into your total loan balance rather than paid in cash at closing.
2. **Annual MIP:** This is an annual fee (usually 0.55% of the loan amount), divided by 12, and added to your monthly mortgage payment. For most buyers putting down 3.5%, this annual MIP stays on the loan for the *entire life of the mortgage*.

## ⚙️ How to Use the FHA Loan Calculator

Calculating an FHA mortgage is more complicated than a standard mortgage because you have to factor in both the Upfront and Annual MIP, as well as property taxes and home insurance.

### 1. Enter the Home Price and Down Payment
Start by entering the purchase price of the home. Select your down payment percentage (e.g., 3.5%). The calculator will automatically determine your base loan amount.

### 2. Enter Interest Rate and Term
Input the interest rate offered by your lender. Most FHA loans are 30-year fixed-rate mortgages.

Our calculator will instantly output your total monthly obligation. It breaks down:
* **Principal & Interest (P&I):** Repaying the actual loan.
