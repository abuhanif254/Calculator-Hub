# The Ultimate Guide to VA Loans: Zero Down, Zero PMI

For active-duty military members, veterans, and eligible surviving spouses, the VA Loan is arguably the most powerful mortgage product in existence. 

Backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs, this loan program eliminates the biggest barriers to homeownership: saving for a down payment and paying punishing monthly insurance fees. In this guide, we will break down exactly how VA loans work and how to use our free [VA Mortgage Calculator](/en/calculators/va-mortgage-calculator) to plan your purchase.

---

## 🎖️ The Big Three Benefits of a VA Loan

If you have earned your VA entitlement, you have access to financial terms that civilians simply cannot get.

1. **Zero Down Payment:** You can finance 100% of the home's purchase price. You do not need to bring a single dollar of down payment to the closing table.
2. **Zero PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance):** With a conventional loan, if you put down 0%, you will be forced to pay hundreds of dollars a month in PMI to protect the bank. Because the government is backing your VA loan, PMI is permanently waived.
3. **Lower Interest Rates:** Because the VA guarantees a portion of the loan, the lender's risk is massively reduced, resulting in interest rates that are typically 0.25% to 0.50% lower than conventional mortgages.

---

## 🛑 The Catch: The VA Funding Fee

While there is no PMI, the VA program is not entirely free. To keep the program running for future generations without relying solely on taxpayer money, the VA charges a one-time "Funding Fee."

* The fee is a percentage of the total loan amount.
* For first-time users putting $0 down, the fee is typically **2.15%**.
* For subsequent users (if you have used a VA loan before), the fee jumps to **3.30%**.
* **Good News:** You do not have to pay this fee out-of-pocket. The vast majority of buyers choose to roll the Funding Fee directly into the total loan balance.

*(Note: Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are completely exempt from the Funding Fee).*
