# Healthy Weight Calculator: Finding Your Ideal Range

With so much conflicting advice online, determining what you "should" weigh can be incredibly stressful. In reality, there is no single perfect number on the scale. 

Instead, medical professionals recommend aiming for a **Healthy Weight Range** based on your height, age, and sex. In this guide, we will explain the science behind weight guidelines, the limitations of BMI, and how to use our free [Healthy Weight Calculator](/en/calculators/healthy-weight-calculator).

---

## ⚖️ The Concept of BMI

The most common method for determining healthy weight is the **Body Mass Index (BMI)**. It is a simple mathematical formula that divides your weight by the square of your height.

**Standard BMI Categories:**
* **Underweight:** BMI < 18.5
* **Normal (Healthy) Weight:** BMI 18.5 – 24.9
* **Overweight:** BMI 25 – 29.9
* **Obese:** BMI 30+

Our calculator uses these BMI thresholds in reverse. It takes your height, plugs in the "Healthy" BMI range of 18.5 to 24.9, and calculates the exact weight in pounds or kilograms that fits into that bracket!

---

## ⚠️ The Limitations of the Scale

While the healthy weight range is a good starting point, it is not perfect. 
The scale only measures *total mass*. It cannot tell the difference between fat, muscle, bone, and water.

* **Muscle Mass:** Muscle is much denser than fat. Athletes often weigh "too much" for their height and fall into the Overweight BMI category, even though they have very low body fat.
* **Age:** Older adults may naturally carry slightly more weight or lose muscle mass, meaning strict BMI charts might not be the healthiest target.

Always consult with a healthcare provider to determine the best target weight for your specific body type and health goals.
