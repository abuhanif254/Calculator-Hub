# Boyle's Law Calculator: Pressure vs. Volume

If you take a balloon and squeeze it tightly between your hands, it will eventually pop. By reducing the volume (squishing it), you are forcing the air molecules inside to hit the rubber walls more frequently, drastically increasing the internal pressure until the rubber breaks.

This inverse relationship between the pressure and volume of a gas was discovered in 1662 and is known as **Boyle's Law**. In this guide, we will explore how it works and how to use our free [Boyle's Law Calculator](/en/calculators/boyles-law-calculator).

---

## 🎈 The Science of Squishing

Boyle's law states that if the temperature and the amount of gas stay completely constant, **Pressure and Volume are inversely proportional**. 
* If you **cut the Volume in half**, the Pressure will **double**.
* If you **triple the Volume**, the Pressure will drop to **one-third**.

**Real World Example:** 
When you pull back the plunger of a syringe, you are increasing the volume inside the plastic tube. According to Boyle's law, this increase in volume causes a massive *drop* in pressure inside the tube. Because the air pressure outside the syringe is now much higher than the pressure inside, fluids are pushed up into the needle!

---

## 🧮 The Boyle's Law Equation

Because they are inversely proportional, if you multiply the Pressure and the Volume of a gas together at any given moment, the answer will always be the exact same constant number.

**The Formula:**
**$P_1V_1 = P_2V_2$**

* **$P_1$ and $V_1$** = The Initial Pressure and Volume.
* **$P_2$ and $V_2$** = The Final Pressure and Volume.

You can use any units for pressure (atm, torr, Pascal) and any units for volume (Liters, mL, gallons) as long as you use the exact same units on both sides of the equation!
