# The Magic of Mortgage Amortization: How to Save $100,000 on Your Home

When you take out a 30-year mortgage, the bank doesn't just divide your total loan by 360 months. They use a mathematical process called **amortization** to heavily front-load the interest payments.

If you don't understand how this schedule works, you will end up paying double the original purchase price of your home in pure interest. In this guide, we will break down the mechanics of amortization and show you how to use our free [Mortgage Amortization Calculator](/en/calculators/mortgage-amortization-calculator) to hack the system and save a fortune.

## 🏦 How Amortization Really Works

An amortized loan guarantees that your total monthly payment remains exactly the same for the entire life of the loan. However, *what* that payment goes toward changes drastically every single month.

**The Early Years (Mostly Interest):**
In the first five years of a standard 30-year mortgage, roughly **70% to 80%** of your monthly payment goes directly into the bank's pocket as interest. Only a tiny fraction goes toward reducing your actual principal balance (the amount you owe on the house).
* *Example:* If your payment is $2,000, only $400 might go to the principal, while $1,600 goes to interest. This is why people sell their house after 5 years and realize they've barely built any equity!

**The Later Years (Mostly Principal):**
As you slowly chip away at the principal over the decades, the bank charges you less interest on the remaining balance. By year 25, the ratio has flipped: most of your payment goes to the principal, and very little goes to interest.

## 💸 The Cheat Code: Extra Principal Payments

Because the bank calculates your interest charge based on your *current outstanding principal balance*, every dollar you pay early destroys future interest mathematically.

If you add just **$100 extra per month** to your mortgage payment (and explicitly tell the bank to apply it to the "Principal Balance"):
1. You bypass the amortization curve.
2. You reduce the balance that next month's interest is calculated on.
3. Over a 30-year loan, that $100/month can easily shave **4 to 5 years** off your mortgage and save you **tens of thousands of dollars** in interest!
