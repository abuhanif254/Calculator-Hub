# Percent Composition: Analyzing Chemical Makeup

Water ($H_2O$) is made of Hydrogen and Oxygen. Because there are two Hydrogen atoms and only one Oxygen atom, you might assume that water is mostly Hydrogen. 

**This is a massive misconception.** Oxygen atoms are significantly heavier than Hydrogen atoms. In reality, water is almost 90% Oxygen by weight! To prove this, chemists use **Percent Composition**. In this guide, we will explain the math and show you how to use our free [Percent Composition Calculator](/en/calculators/percent-composition-calculator).

---

## 🔬 What is Percent Composition?

Percent composition tells you exactly how much of a chemical compound's total mass comes from each individual element. It is crucial for analytical chemistry, forensic science, and even nutrition (determining how much actual iron is inside an iron supplement).

**The Formula:**
**% Element = (Mass of Element / Total Molar Mass) × 100**

---

## 🧮 Step-by-Step Example: Water ($H_2O$)

Let's do the math to prove that water is mostly Oxygen by weight.

**Step 1: Find the Total Molar Mass**
* H (Hydrogen) = 1.008 g/mol. Since there are 2: `1.008 × 2 = 2.016 g/mol`
* O (Oxygen) = 15.999 g/mol.
* Total Mass of Water = `2.016 + 15.999 = 18.015 g/mol`.

**Step 2: Calculate the Percentage for Hydrogen**
* (Mass of H / Total Mass) × 100
* (2.016 / 18.015) × 100 = **11.19% Hydrogen**

**Step 3: Calculate the Percentage for Oxygen**
* (Mass of O / Total Mass) × 100
* (15.999 / 18.015) × 100 = **88.81% Oxygen**

Even though there are twice as many Hydrogen atoms, Oxygen dominates the weight of the molecule!
