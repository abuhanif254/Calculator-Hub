# Understanding Density: Why Ice Floats

Which is heavier: a pound of feathers or a pound of lead? 
They both weigh exactly one pound, so their Mass is identical. However, the pound of feathers takes up the size of a pillow, while the pound of lead takes up the size of a golf ball. The difference between the two is their **Density**.

In this guide, we will break down the formula for density, explain buoyancy, and show you how to use our free [Density Calculator](/en/calculators/density-calculator).

---

## 📦 What is Density?

Density is a measure of how tightly packed the matter in an object is. 
* **Mass:** How much "stuff" is in the object.
* **Volume:** How much physical space the object takes up.

If you take a piece of bread and crush it into a tiny, hard ball, you haven't changed its Mass (it still has the same amount of bread). But you *have* drastically reduced its Volume. Because the same amount of mass is now packed into a much smaller space, the bread's Density has increased!

**The Equation:** *Density = Mass / Volume (d = m/v)*

---

## 🧊 Why Does Ice Float?

Usually, when a substance freezes into a solid, its molecules pack tighter together, making it *more* dense than its liquid form. 
Water is a bizarre, miraculous exception. 

When water freezes into ice, the water molecules arrange themselves into a rigid crystal lattice that actually takes up *more* space than liquid water. Because the Volume increases but the Mass stays the same, the Density of ice drops. 

Since ice is less dense than liquid water, it floats! If water behaved like most other liquids, ice would sink, oceans would freeze from the bottom up, and all marine life on Earth would die. 

---

## 🎈 Hot Air Balloons and Buoyancy

Density also applies to gases. When you heat up air, the air molecules gain energy and spread further apart, increasing their Volume. This causes the heated air to become *less dense* than the colder air around it. 
Because less dense things float on top of more dense things, the hot air rises, lifting the hot air balloon into the sky!
