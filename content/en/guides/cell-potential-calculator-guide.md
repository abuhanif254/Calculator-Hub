# Cell Potential Calculator: Predicting Spontaneous Reactions

Have you ever wondered why some batteries last forever while others die almost immediately? Or why iron rusts but gold stays shiny for thousands of years?

The answer lies in **Electrochemistry** — the science of converting chemical energy into electrical energy (and vice versa). The key measure of how powerful an electrochemical cell is, is called its **Cell Potential**. In this guide, we will learn what it means and how to use our free [Cell Potential Calculator](/en/calculators/cell-potential-calculator).

---

## ⚡ What is Cell Potential?

Every metal has a different tendency to lose electrons (oxidize). Some metals, like zinc, give up electrons very easily. Others, like gold or platinum, hold onto their electrons extremely tightly.

**Standard Reduction Potential ($E^\circ$)** is the measurement of how strongly a substance "wants" to gain electrons (be reduced), measured under standard conditions. A table of these values is called a **Standard Reduction Potential Table**.

**Cell Potential** ($E^\circ_{cell}$) tells you how much electrical voltage a specific electrochemical cell will produce. A positive cell potential means the reaction is **spontaneous** (it happens on its own, like in a battery). A negative cell potential means the reaction is non-spontaneous (it requires energy input, like in electrolysis).

---

## 🧮 The Cell Potential Equation

To calculate the standard cell potential, you identify which substance is oxidized (loses electrons — the anode) and which is reduced (gains electrons — the cathode), and then use their standard reduction potentials.

**The Formula:**
**$E^\circ_{cell} = E^\circ_{cathode} - E^\circ_{anode}$**

*Trick to Remember:* Think of it as **"Cathode minus Anode"** or **"Reduction minus Oxidation"**.

**Example:** A classic Zinc-Copper cell:
* $E^\circ$ for $\text{Cu}^{2+}/\text{Cu}$ (cathode) = $+0.34 \text{ V}$
* $E^\circ$ for $\text{Zn}^{2+}/\text{Zn}$ (anode) = $-0.76 \text{ V}$
* $E^\circ_{cell} = +0.34 - (-0.76) = +1.10 \text{ V}$ ✅ Spontaneous!
