# Power Supply: How Much Wattage Do You Need?

When building a custom PC, it is tempting to spend all your money on a high-end graphics card (GPU) and a fast processor (CPU), leaving the Power Supply Unit (PSU) as an afterthought. This is a dangerous mistake!

If your Power Supply cannot deliver enough clean, consistent wattage to your components, your system will randomly crash, reboot, or in worst-case scenarios, catch fire. 

In this guide, we will explain how to calculate your system's power draw, the importance of the 80 PLUS efficiency rating, and how to use our free [Power Supply Calculator](/en/calculators/power-supply-calculator).

---

## ⚡ Calculating Total System Draw

Every component inside a computer consumes electrical power (measured in Watts). To find out how big your Power Supply needs to be, you must add up the maximum power draw (TDP) of every single part:

* **CPU:** Usually between 65W and 250W.
* **GPU (Graphics Card):** The biggest power hog, ranging from 150W up to 600W for flagship cards.
* **Motherboard & RAM:** About 40W to 80W total.
* **Storage (SSD/HDD):** About 5W to 10W each.
* **Fans & RGB:** About 3W to 5W each.

If you add all these up and your total is **450W**, you cannot just buy a 450W power supply! You need headroom.

---

## 🛑 The "20% Headroom" Rule

Power supplies are most efficient and stable when they are running at around 50% to 80% of their maximum capacity. If you run a PSU at 100% capacity constantly, its fan will sound like a jet engine, it will generate massive amounts of heat, and its lifespan will drastically decrease.

**The Rule:** Always buy a Power Supply that is at least 20% to 30% larger than your system's maximum draw. 
If your PC needs 450W, you should buy a **600W** PSU.

---

## 🏅 What is the "80 PLUS" Rating?

When converting AC power from your wall outlet into DC power for your PC components, a power supply loses some energy as heat. 
An "80 PLUS" certification guarantees that the power supply is at least 80% efficient at converting that power.
* **80 PLUS White/Bronze:** Standard efficiency (Good for budget builds).
* **80 PLUS Gold:** High efficiency (The sweet spot for most gamers).
* **80 PLUS Platinum/Titanium:** Extreme efficiency (Best for servers running 24/7).
