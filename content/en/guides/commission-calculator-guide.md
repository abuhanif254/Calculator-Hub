# Commission Calculator: Track Your Sales Earnings

If you work in real estate, car sales, or B2B software, your base salary is probably only a small part of your total income. The real money comes from **Commissions**—a percentage of the revenue you generate for the company.

Understanding your exact commission structure is critical for predicting your monthly income. In this guide, we will explain the different types of commission structures, how to calculate them, and how to use our free [Commission Calculator](/en/calculators/commission-calculator).

---

## 💰 Common Commission Structures

Not all companies pay commissions the same way. The two most common structures are:

### 1. Flat Rate Commission
This is the simplest structure. You earn a fixed percentage of every sale you make, regardless of how much you sell in a month.
* *Example:* You earn a flat 5% on all software sales. If you sell \$100,000 worth of software, you take home \$5,000.

### 2. Tiered (Graduated) Commission
To encourage salespeople to hustle harder, companies often use tiered structures. As you hit higher revenue milestones, your commission percentage increases!
* *Example:* 
  * 5% on the first \$50,000 in sales.
  * 8% on sales between \$50,001 and \$100,000.
  * 12% on all sales above \$100,000.

---

## 🧮 How to Calculate Your Commission

For a flat rate, the math is simple:
**Commission = Total Sales $\times$ Commission Rate**

*Example:* \$50,000 in sales at a 6% rate.
$\text{Commission} = 50,000 \times 0.06 = \$3,000$

For tiered structures, you must slice your total sales into brackets and calculate the commission for each bracket separately, then add them all together. (This is exactly how income tax brackets work!)
