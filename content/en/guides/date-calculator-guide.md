## 🗓️ The Quirks of the Calendar

Date math is uniquely difficult because our calendar is not base-10 like our standard number system. 
* A week is 7 days.
* A month can be 28, 29, 30, or 31 days.
* A year is 365 days—unless it's a leap year!

### What is a Leap Year?
A true solar year (the exact time it takes the Earth to orbit the sun) is approximately **365.2422 days**. If we only had 365 days in a calendar year, the seasons would slowly drift over centuries. To fix this, we add a "leap day" (February 29th) almost every four years.
* **The Rule:** A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4. However, century years (like 1900) are *not* leap years unless they are also divisible by 400 (like the year 2000).

## ➕ Adding and Subtracting Dates

If someone asks you, "What is the date 45 days from today?", you can't just do simple arithmetic. You have to figure out how many days are in the current month, subtract that, roll over to the next month, and potentially account for a leap year.

**Business Days vs. Calendar Days**
In the corporate and legal world, dates are often calculated in "Business Days" (Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays).
* If a contract gives you "30 Calendar Days" to respond, weekends count.
* If a contract gives you "30 Business Days", it actually takes about 42 real days to hit the deadline!
