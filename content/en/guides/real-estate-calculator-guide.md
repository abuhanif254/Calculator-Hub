# Real Estate ROI: How to Analyze an Investment Property

Buying a rental property is one of the most proven paths to wealth, but buying the *wrong* property can drain your savings. 

To succeed in real estate investing, you must detach your emotions and look strictly at the math. In this guide, we will explore the three most important metrics every investor must know, and how to use our [Real Estate Calculator](/en/calculators/real-estate-calculator) to evaluate your next deal.

---

## 🏢 The 3 Golden Metrics of Real Estate

### 1. Net Operating Income (NOI)
Before calculating any returns, you must know how much cash the property generates after expenses.
* **Formula:** Gross Rental Income - Operating Expenses
* *Note:* Operating expenses include taxes, insurance, maintenance, and property management. It does **not** include your mortgage payment (debt service).

### 2. Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate)
The Cap Rate tells you the rate of return you would get on the property if you bought it in **all cash** (no mortgage). It is the best metric for comparing the profitability of different properties in the same market.
* **Formula:** NOI / Purchase Price
* *Example:* A property generating $10,000 in NOI that costs $100,000 has a 10% Cap Rate.

### 3. Cash on Cash Return (CoC)
Because most people use a mortgage to buy real estate, the Cap Rate doesn't tell the whole story. Cash on Cash return measures the annual return you make on the *actual cash you invested* (your down payment and closing costs).
* **Formula:** Annual Pre-Tax Cash Flow / Total Cash Invested
* *Example:* If you put down $20,000, and the property profits $2,000 a year (after paying the mortgage), your CoC return is 10%.
