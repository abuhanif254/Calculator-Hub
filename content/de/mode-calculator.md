## Section 1: Deep Dive into Frequency Distributions

The mode is a measure of central tendency that represents the most frequently occurring value in a dataset. While the mean and median provide a mathematical center, the mode indicates the most common or popular item. The store needs to know the mode to order stock accurately, as ordering "size 9.2" shoes is impossible.
