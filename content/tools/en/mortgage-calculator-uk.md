### How UK Mortgage Repayments Work: Repayment vs. Interest-Only

#### 1. Capital & Interest (Repayment) Mortgages
The vast majority of UK residential mortgages are **repayment mortgages**. Each monthly direct debit covers two components:
1. **Monthly Interest Charge:** Calculated on your remaining loan balance ($\text{Balance} \times \frac{\text{Annual Rate}}{12}$).
2. **Capital Reduction:** The portion of your payment that reduces the principal balance owed to the lender.

In the early years of a 25-year or 30-year mortgage, interest makes up the largest share of your monthly payment. As the principal shrinks, an increasing proportion of each payment goes directly toward clearing your debt, guaranteeing the loan reaches **£0** at the end of the term.

#### 2. Interest-Only Mortgages
Commonly used for **Buy-to-Let (BTL)** investments and specialist residential lending, an interest-only mortgage requires you to pay only the monthly interest charge:
$$\text{Monthly Interest-Only Payment} = \frac{\text{Loan Amount} \times \text{Annual Interest Rate}}{12}$$
While monthly payments are significantly lower, the original loan balance remains unchanged at the end of the mortgage term and must be repaid in full via a verified repayment vehicle, property sale, or refinancing.

### Understanding Loan-to-Value (LTV) Tiers in the UK Market

UK mortgage lenders price their fixed and tracker products according to **Loan-to-Value (LTV) bands**—the ratio of your mortgage loan to the appraised property value:
$$\text{LTV (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Property Price} - \text{Deposit}}{\text{Property Price}}\right) \times 100$$

* **90% – 95% LTV (5%–10% Deposit):** Designed for first-time buyers; carries the highest interest rates due to lower equity buffer.
* **80% – 85% LTV (15%–20% Deposit):** Unlocks noticeably lower rates and broader lender product availability.
* **75% LTV (25% Deposit):** A major UK lending threshold (especially for Buy-to-Let and remortgaging) where competitive sub-benchmark rates become available.
* **60% LTV or Lower (40%+ Deposit):** Qualifies for lenders' lowest "headline" prime residential mortgage rates.

> **Tip:** If your deposit puts you at `76% LTV` or `81% LTV`, contributing a small additional lump sum to drop into the `75%` or `80%` LTV band can reduce the interest rate charged on the **entire** loan balance, saving thousands of pounds over a 2-year or 5-year fixed period.

### Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) & Upfront UK Buying Costs

When budgeting for a property purchase in England and Northern Ireland (or LBTT in Scotland / LTT in Wales), factor in these mandatory upfront costs alongside your deposit:
1. **Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT):** Charged on tiered property price slices above the residential nil-rate threshold, with higher rates for additional properties / Buy-to-Let purchases and relief thresholds for eligible First-Time Buyers.
2. **Product / Arrangement Fee:** Typically **£999 to £1,499** on lowest-rate fixed deals. Adding this fee to the mortgage loan incurs compound interest for the full term; paying it upfront avoids interest charges.
3. **Valuation, RICS Survey & Conveyancing:** Budget **£1,500 to £3,000** for legal searches, Land Registry fees, and a Level 2 HomeBuyer or Level 3 Building Survey.

### How Overpayments Cut Years Off Your UK Mortgage

Most UK fixed-rate mortgages permit **penalty-free overpayments of up to 10% of the outstanding balance per year** without triggering an Early Repayment Charge (ERC). Because every £1 overpaid reduces your capital balance immediately:
* Future monthly interest is calculated on a smaller principal.
* Regular monthly overpayments of even **£100 to £250 per month** can shave **3 to 6 years** off a 25-year term and reduce your LTV faster so you qualify for a cheaper rate band when you remortgage.
