## Typographic Mechanics: Why Character Cadence Beats Repeated Copy

Standard Lorem Ipsum traces back to sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of Cicero's 45 BC treatise *De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum*, scrambled by sixteenth-century type specimen printers and preserved across five centuries of typesetting. Its durability in digital product design stems from quantitative letter and word distribution rather than nostalgia:

- **Natural Rag and Word-Length Variance**: The classic 63-token corpus mixes short particles (`et`, `in`, `ut`, `do`) with polysyllabic words (`consectetur`, `reprehenderit`, `exercitation`, `incididunt`). That mix approximates the statistical word-length variance of English and Western European prose, allowing right-ragged paragraphs, justified columns, and automated CSS hyphenation to behave realistically.
- **Measure Calibration (45–75 Characters Per Line)**: Typographic readability research establishes an optimal line length (measure) between 45 and 75 characters—roughly `60ch` to `68ch` in CSS, where `1ch` equals the advance width of the current font's `0` glyph rather than an arbitrary pixel breakpoint. Generating multi-paragraph dummy blocks lets designers verify that body copy never stretches across ultra-wide monitors without container clamping and that `line-height` stays comfortably in the `1.5–1.65` range across responsive viewport widths.
- **Zero Semantic Distraction**: Because scrambled Latin carries no literal meaning to most reviewers, cognitive attention stays anchored on vertical rhythm, heading-to-body contrast ratio, optical kerning, and whitespace balance rather than premature copyediting.

## Stress-Testing CSS Line Clamping, Measure, and Overflow

Generating both short (1-sentence) and extreme (1,000-character) dummy blocks helps verify that responsive UI cards remain uniform across uneven content lengths. Pair generated placeholder text with defensive CSS rules so unbreakable long tokens never blow out flexbox or grid tracks:

```css
.article-card__excerpt {
  /* Constrain measure for readability */
  max-width: 65ch;
  line-height: 1.6;

  /* Prevent long unspaced tokens from overflowing flex/grid items */
  min-width: 0;
  overflow-wrap: anywhere;
  hyphens: auto;

  /* Standard 3-line truncation with ellipsis */
  display: -webkit-box;
  -webkit-box-orient: vertical;
  -webkit-line-clamp: 3;
  overflow: hidden;
}
```

Testing `.article-card__excerpt` with the `Tech` or `Developer` corpus at 50 words and again at 500 characters immediately confirms whether card heights stay aligned and whether `min-width: 0` prevents horizontal scrollbar leaks inside CSS Grid tracks.
